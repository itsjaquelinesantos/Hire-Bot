# Hire Bot

**Hire Bot** is an educational project developed collaboratively with a **second-year high school class** as part of a technology project focused on programming, Artificial Intelligence, and the future of work.

The website simulates an **AI recruitment chat**, where the user interacts with a virtual recruiter and answers a series of questions as if they were participating in a job interview.

## About the Project

The main idea behind Hire Bot is to create a **simulation of an AI recruiter while preserving the personality, creativity, and ideas of the students who developed it**.

Instead of simply following a fixed script, the project was designed so that the students themselves could create the bot's interactions. Each student contributed by creating their own **questions, possible answers, and reactions to those answers**, giving the simulated AI its own personality and style.

The project combines **programming logic with human creativity**, showing that technology and Artificial Intelligence can be used as tools to express ideas created by people.

## How It Works

During the simulated interview, the user interacts with the Hire Bot and chooses answers to different questions.

Each answer is connected to a scoring system that represents different professional areas. Based on the accumulated points throughout the interview, the bot suggests one of three possible areas:

* **Technology**
* **Healthcare**
* **Administration & Business**

The final result represents the professional area that received the highest score based on the user's answers.

## AI Simulation

Although Hire Bot simulates an AI recruiter, it does **not use a real Artificial Intelligence model**.

The interaction is based on **predefined questions, answers, reactions, and scoring rules created by the students**. This was an intentional part of the project: the students were able to understand how an AI-like interaction can be constructed through programming logic while adding their own creativity and personality to the experience.

This makes the project a combination of:

**Human creativity + Programming + AI simulation**

## Educational Purpose

Hire Bot was created to explore the relationship between **technology, Artificial Intelligence, and the future of work**.

Through the project, students had the opportunity to think about:

* How AI can simulate human interactions
* How algorithms can make decisions based on user input
* How programming logic can create interactive experiences
* The role of AI in recruitment and professional environments
* The importance of human creativity when developing technology
* Different career areas and their relationship with technology
